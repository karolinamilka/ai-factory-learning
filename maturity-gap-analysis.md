## Gap Analysis

### Gap 1

**Dimension:** Performance Tracking
**Current level:** [L1]
**Why this gap is most damaging:** Without shared and reusable AI assets, knowledge remains with individuals, resulting in duplicated effort, inconsistent outputs, and slower onboarding of team members.
**Root cause:** The team has not defined measurable AI adoption or productivity metrics, so AI usage is evaluated informally rather than through objective evidence.

---

### Gap 2

**Dimension:** Reusability
**Current level:** [L1]
**Why this gap is most damaging:** Without measurable success metrics, the team cannot demonstrate the value of AI adoption, prioritize improvements, or make evidence-based decisions about expanding AI usage.
**Root cause:** There is no shared repository, ownership model, or team practice for creating, maintaining, and reusing AI prompts or other AI assets.
